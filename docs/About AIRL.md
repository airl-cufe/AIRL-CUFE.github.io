# About AIRL

The CUFE Artificial Intelligence Research Lab (AIRL) is a team of staff members, and resources dedicated to supporting the students and researchers with computational power needed for AI development. The team also connects industrial partners with researchers to create a sustainable environment for R&D projects that target the industrial challenges.

AIRL's mission has four elements:

- providing access to computational resources for all members of the Cairo University's Faculty of Engineering (CUFE) community at no charge
- working with industrial partners to discuss thier challenges, identify aspects of improvement, and develop applicable solutions fitting their needs.
- working with principal investigators (PIs) to support their specialized high performance computational needs
- providing students from different engineering departments with the learning materials, computational resources, and mentoring support for their career development in the AI fields.

AIRL is established in July 2026. Prof. Ahmed Morsy created the lab with funding from Ezz Steel group, making high performance computational resources available to the research community.
