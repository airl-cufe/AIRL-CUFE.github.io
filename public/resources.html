Scratch

Temporary datasets, checkpoints, and intermediate outputs. Files older than 7 days may be automatically deleted.

!
Confirm project-storage backup status before relying on it.

The supplied Resources overview lists project storage as not backed up; the governance regulations describe project storage as backed up. Ask the lab administration which policy is current. Users should always maintain independent backups.

INTERACTIVE SESSION DEFAULT2 hours · 1 GPU · 16 GB RAM

Use interactive sessions for development, debugging, visualization, and small experiments. Submit longer work as batch jobs.

LARGE DATASETSOver 100 GB needs approval

Get lab-administrator approval before uploading large datasets. Prefer shared read-only copies of public datasets when practical.

RESEARCH DATALow-security data only

Do not store personal, financial, or sensitive intellectual-property data, or data subject to NDAs, national-security restrictions, or controls requiring security audits.
