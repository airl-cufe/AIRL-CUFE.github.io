ABOUT THE LAB

Research needs
room to grow.

The CUFE Artificial Intelligence Research Lab (AIRL) brings people and computing resources together to support AI research, education, and industry-facing innovation.

Home/ About AIRL
OUR PURPOSE

Four ways we
move research forward.

AIRL supports the Faculty of Engineering community with shared compute, specialist support, and connections that help turn research questions into useful work.

01 / ACCESS

Open computing for CUFE

Provide Cairo University's Faculty of Engineering community with access to AI and high-performance computing resources at no charge.

02 / INDUSTRY

Connect research and industry

Work with industrial partners to understand challenges, identify opportunities, and develop applicable AI solutions.

03 / RESEARCH

Support principal investigators

Help PIs meet specialized high-performance computing needs for their research programs.

04 / EDUCATION

Help students build skills

Provide students across engineering departments with learning materials, computing resources, and mentoring for careers in AI.

ESTABLISHEDJuly 2026

Prof. Ahmed Morsy established AIRL with funding from Ezz Steel Group, bringing high-performance computing resources to the research community.
